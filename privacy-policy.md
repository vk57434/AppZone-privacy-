# Privacy Policy for AppZone
**Effective Date: September 2, 2026**

AppZone ("AppZone", "we", "us", or "our") is an Android application marketplace that allows users to discover, download, review, and manage applications and allows approved developers to publish and manage their applications.

This Privacy Policy explains how AppZone collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the AppZone application and related services.

By using AppZone, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and Authentication Information
When you create or sign in to an AppZone account, we may collect:

- Name or display name
- Email address
- Profile photo, if provided
- Firebase authentication identifier
- Account role, such as user or developer
- Account creation and update information
AppZone may support authentication through Google and Firebase Authentication. Authentication providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

### 1.2 Profile Information
If you choose to create or update your profile, AppZone may collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Profile picture
- Developer-related information when you apply for or use a developer account
Profile pictures may be selected from your device's gallery or captured using your device camera.

### 1.3 Developer Information
Users applying to become developers may provide additional information such as:

- Company name (for example: Max Technologies & Pvt Ltd)
- Application or business description
- Reason for requesting developer access
- Developer name (if required by the platform, for example: Vivek)
This information may be reviewed by AppZone administrators for developer-account approval and platform administration.

### 1.4 App and Usage Information
To provide AppZone's marketplace functionality, we may process information related to your use of the service, including:

- Applications viewed
- Recently viewed applications
- Favorite applications
- Applications downloaded through AppZone
- Application reviews and ratings
- Downloaded application package names and versions
- Application installation/update status where required for marketplace functionality
- App interaction and preference information
Download records may be associated with your authenticated account so that AppZone can maintain download statistics, prevent duplicate download records, and provide marketplace functionality.

### 1.5 Reviews and Public Content
If you submit an application review, rating, or comment, information associated with the review may be stored and displayed to other AppZone users.

Depending on your account information and the functionality available, your display name and profile photo may appear alongside your review.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in public reviews or other content that you submit.

### 1.6 Application and Developer Content
Developers may upload application-related information and files, including:

- Application name
- Package name
- Company name: Max Technologies & Pvt Ltd
- Developer name (if required): Vivek
- Description
- Category
- Version information
- Application icon
- Screenshots
- Release notes
- APK/application files
- Application file metadata such as size and cryptographic hash
Uploaded applications may be reviewed by AppZone administrators before being made available to users.

## 2. Camera and Photos
AppZone may request access to the device camera when you choose to take a profile picture.

AppZone may also allow you to select a picture from your device's gallery.

Camera or gallery access is used only when you initiate a relevant profile-photo action. AppZone does not use the camera to continuously monitor you.

A selected profile image may be uploaded to our backend or associated storage service so that it can be displayed as your profile picture.

You can remove your profile picture through the application where this functionality is available.

## 3. Application Installation and Device Information
AppZone provides functionality for downloading and installing Android applications.

The application may inspect installed application/package information on the device to determine whether applications are already installed and to provide appropriate installation or update functionality.

AppZone may request Android permissions related to package visibility and application installation to provide these features.

We do not claim ownership of applications installed on your device. Applications downloaded through AppZone may have their own privacy policies and data practices, which are separate from AppZone.

You should review the privacy policy and permissions of an application before installing or using it.
